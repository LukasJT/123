# Content policy

Every new factual record needs a stable ID, media type, immutable URL, title, year, verification date, batch ID, and at least one authoritative source. Unknown facts remain empty. Release status, runtime, completion status, credits, availability, ratings, and trend claims require a named source and checked date.

Editorial guides must answer a distinct viewing question, state their selection logic, avoid major spoilers, and explain why each recommendation belongs. Templates may control presentation but cannot create thin variants by swapping keywords. 123Videos does not claim first-hand viewing unless a real named contributor supplies it.

The site is an informational catalog. Do not use “watch free,” playback quality, or streaming availability language without the corresponding service and evidence. Do not add VideoObject markup to catalog pages. Ads remain labeled and sponsored links use appropriate rel attributes.
